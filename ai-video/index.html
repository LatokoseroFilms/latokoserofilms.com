AI video

Generative shots built into a real production pipeline — planned at previz, finished by hand, and integrated so they read as part of the film.

Where it actually helps

Set extension
a rooftop becomes a skyline, a room becomes a hall.
Crowd and background
a dozen extras become a stadium.
Impossible locations
places you can’t get a permit for, or can’t fly to.
Concept and previz
boards and animatics in hours instead of days, so you approve the idea earlier.
Versioning
one shoot, many market variants, without a reshoot.

Where it doesn’t

We won’t use it for a hero performance, a close-up on a face that has to carry emotion, or anything where a client’s product has to be reproduced exactly. Those get shot. Being clear about this is the point.

Our pipeline

  1. Concept

    Generative boards and animatics from the script.

  2. Previz

    We decide which shots are generative before the shoot, not after.

  3. Plate

    Where a generative element sits inside live action, we shoot the plate properly. Good comp starts on set.

  4. Generation

    Shots built, iterated, and reviewed against the boards.

  5. Comp and grade

    A compositor and colorist match grain, lens character and color so the shot sits in the cut.

  6. Finish

    Delivered with the rest of the film. Same masters, same specs.

Rights and disclosure

  • You own the delivered film. Contract states it plainly.
  • We don’t train models on your material, and we don’t reuse your footage on other clients’ work.
  • We’ll tell you which shots are generative, and we’ll put it in writing if your broadcaster, platform or brand guidelines require disclosure.
  • We don’t generate likenesses of real people without written consent from that person.

Featured work

Ask us for a shot breakdown — we’ll walk you through a generative shot frame by frame, including the plate it was built on.

What it costs

Generative shots are quoted per shot, inside the wider production budget. For specific shots the saving is substantial; for a whole film the value is in what you no longer have to cut from the script.

Questions

Is it cheaper?

For specific shots, substantially. For a whole film, no — the savings are in what you no longer have to cut from the script.

Will it look like AI?

Not if it’s planned at previz and finished by a compositor. It looks like AI when it’s bolted on at the end.

Do you disclose it to audiences?

We’ll do whatever your brand, platform or regulator requires, and we’ll advise you on what that is.

Can you match an existing brand film’s look?

Yes — send the reference and we’ll test before you commit.
