Commercials

Brand films, campaign spots and product videos for Nepali and regional brands — written, shot and finished by one team.

What’s included

  • Concept development and scripting
  • Storyboards and shot list
  • Casting, location scouting, permits
  • Direction, cinematography, crew and kit
  • Edit, grade, sound design and mix
  • Cutdowns for TV, YouTube, Instagram and in-app placement

Process

  1. Brief and budget

    We start from what the campaign has to achieve, not from a shot idea.

  2. Concept

    Two or three routes, one page each. You pick one; we develop it.

  3. Previz

    Boards and a shot list. Nothing goes to camera unapproved.

  4. Production

    Typically one to three shoot days for a 30–60 second spot.

  5. Post and delivery

    First cut within a week of wrap. Two revision rounds included. Delivered in every aspect ratio your media plan needs.

Questions

How long from brief to delivery?

Three to five weeks for a standard spot. Faster is possible; it costs more.

Do you handle casting and permits?

Yes, both, including Kathmandu location permissions.

Can you work from an agency’s script?

Yes. We’re often the production partner on an agency-led campaign.

How many revisions?

Two rounds in the base scope. Further rounds are billed at a day rate agreed upfront.

Do we own the footage?

You own the delivered film and the licensed usage. Raw rushes are available as an add-on — we’ll state which at contract.

Tell us what you’re making

Send a brief and you’ll hear back within two working days — with a realistic budget range, not a placeholder.
